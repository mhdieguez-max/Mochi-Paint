Help & Support

Mochi Paint is provided by CyberPrep Solutions LLC. Answers to common questions are below.

Coloring basics

Pick a character from the home screen, tap a color, then tap the picture to fill areas — or draw with the pencil, marker, crayon, and spray tools. Undo and redo fix slips. Everything works offline once the app is installed.

Saving your artwork

Coloring progress saves automatically on your device — leave a page and come back anytime. Tap Save to export a finished picture as a PNG image (it lands in your device's Downloads or Files app), and Print to print it. Saved PNGs are yours to keep, share, or print.

Artwork stays on your device

Mochi Paint has no cloud and no accounts, so coloring progress does not transfer to a new device and cannot be recovered if the device is reset or the app's storage is cleared. To keep a picture forever, export it with Save — the PNG file survives independently of the app.

New device or reinstalling

The app re-installs free on any device signed in to the Google account that bought it, from your Google Play library. Coloring progress starts fresh on the new device; bring finished art along by saving PNGs first.

Price, purchase, and refunds

Mochi Paint is a one-time purchase on Google Play; the exact price in your currency is shown by Google Play before you buy. There are no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases — ever. Refund requests go through Google Play's refund process, subject to Google's policies and your local consumer law. For duplicate charges or purchase problems Google can't resolve, email us with your Google Play order number — never send card details.

Privacy and data

The app collects nothing. See the Privacy Policy and Data Deletion pages.
