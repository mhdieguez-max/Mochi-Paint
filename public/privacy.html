Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Mochi Paint is a coloring app designed for all ages, including children. We keep it simple: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no behavioral tracking, and no cloud gallery.

What stays on your device

  • Your coloring progress and app preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • The Save button creates a PNG file on your device. Mochi Paint does not upload or share it.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes its locally saved progress.

What the app does not collect

  • No names, account details, email addresses, passwords, or user profiles.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising ID, or cross-app identifiers.
  • No artwork, analytics events, advertising data, or third-party tracking data.

Website delivery

Like any website, Mochi Paint's hosting provider processes standard connection information, such as an IP address, only as needed to deliver the app securely. Mochi Paint does not use that information to identify users, build profiles, advertise, or track activity across apps or websites.
