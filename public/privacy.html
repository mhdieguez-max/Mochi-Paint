🍡Mochi Paint

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Mochi Paint ("we", "us", "our") is a coloring app developed and operated by the Mochi Paint team, designed for users of all ages, including children. We keep it simple: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no behavioral tracking, and no cloud server storage.

What stays on your device

  • Your coloring progress and app preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • Tapping the Save button creates a PNG file stored directly in your device's local photo gallery or file storage. Mochi Paint uses this access solely to save your artwork — it never reads, uploads, or shares your files.
  • Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app permanently removes its locally saved progress.

What the app does not collect

  • No names, account details, email addresses, passwords, or user profiles.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, advertising IDs (AAID), IP tracking, or cross-app identifiers.
  • No artwork, usage analytics, crash logs, advertising data, or third-party tracking data.
  • No third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs are included in the app at all.

Children's privacy (COPPA & GDPR)

Because Mochi Paint is designed for families and children, we strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the General Data Protection Regulation as it applies to children (GDPR-K), and the Google Play Families Policy. Mochi Paint does not knowingly collect, store, or share any personal information from children — or from any other users. Every user, of every age, receives the same fully private, child-safe experience. Parents and guardians can contact us any time at support@mochipaint.com with privacy questions or deletion requests.

Website delivery

Like any website, Mochi Paint's hosting provider briefly processes standard connection information, such as an IP address, strictly as needed to deliver the app securely. Mochi Paint never uses that information to identify users, build profiles, serve ads, or track activity across apps or websites.

Deleting local data

Because all data is stored exclusively on your local device, deleting it is as simple as clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. See the data deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a parent privacy inquiry? Email support@mochipaint.com.
